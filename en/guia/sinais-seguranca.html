Security signals

Lightweight telemetry and interpretation limits.

Observed signals

proctoringpolicy_securitysignals observes lightweight signals related to client integrity changes and possible use of developer tools.

Pulses and performance

The policy may record periodic pulses when suspicious conditions appear; the interval is configurable. Test load and network quality before changing the frequency.

False positives

Heuristics are not proof of fraud, cheating or AI use, and can also miss real changes. Responsible assessment requires human review.
