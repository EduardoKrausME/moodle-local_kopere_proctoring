Focus and tab switching

Focus changes, configurable thresholds and false positives.

How it works

The proctoringpolicy_focus subplugin observes browser signals for lost focus or hidden pages, including tab or application switches, and supports customized instructions and warnings.

Event threshold

The policy allows a per-quiz focus-change threshold. Validate behavior in real browsers because notifications, assistive tools and legitimate windows can trigger events.

Human interpretation

Losing focus does not reveal the content of another window and is not, by itself, evidence of cheating. Review the event sequence and student explanations.
