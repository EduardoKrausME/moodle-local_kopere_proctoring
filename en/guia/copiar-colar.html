Copy, paste and printing

Browser controls and impacts on answers.

Intercepted events

proctoringpolicy_copy attempts to block copy, cut, paste and right-click actions within the exam page. Students receive advance guidance, and warning messages are configurable.

Browser limits

These controls do not prevent copying using operating-system features, other devices or external methods. They are not unbreakable restrictions.

Legitimate use

Review exams that require code snippets, formulas, accessibility features or authorized pasting. Warning limits do not automatically identify cheating.
