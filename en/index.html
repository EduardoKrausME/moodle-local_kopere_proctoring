How it works

From setup to review.

01

Configure

Choose policies and thresholds in a Quiz.

02

Explain rules

Students complete preparation requirements.

03

Review events

Investigate events, failures and context before deciding.

Focus loss, fullscreen exits, paste operations or integrity alerts do not prove cheating or AI use. Consider accessibility and technical failures.

OPTIONAL INTEGRATION

Proctoring and Moodle MCP.

With local_mcp installed, inspect policies, attempts and events through READ tools, and enable or disable monitoring with WRITE previews and confirmation. MCP is not required to run exams.
