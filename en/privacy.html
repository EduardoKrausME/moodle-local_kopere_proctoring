Privacy and accountability

How institutions should handle monitored-attempt records.

Collected data

Depending on enabled policies, the plugin may store acceptance, timestamps, events, IP addresses, user agents, screen resolution and optional textual geolocation.

Institutional responsibility

Define purpose, lawful basis, transparency, retention, least-privilege access and appeal procedures under the applicable regulations.

Moodle facilities

The privacy provider implements export and deletion in authorized contexts, including evidence files when present.

Interpretation

Browser records are technical observations, not automatic proof of misconduct, cheating or AI authorship.
